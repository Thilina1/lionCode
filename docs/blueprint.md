# **App Name**: Lion Code Technologies

## Core Features:

- Home Page Showcase: A visually engaging landing page to showcase the company's mission, values, and expertise in business automation and IT solutions.
- Services Overview: Detailed pages outlining the specific IT services offered, with clear explanations of how each service solves business problems and automates processes for Sri Lankan businesses.
- Case Studies: A portfolio of successful case studies demonstrating the impact of Lion Code Technologies' solutions on real-world Sri Lankan businesses.
- AI-Powered Chatbot: Implement a tool powered by a large language model, answering frequently asked questions and directing potential clients to relevant services or information.
- Team Introduction: Introduce the core team members with profiles highlighting their expertise and experience in software development and business automation.
- Contact Form: A user-friendly contact form that allows potential clients to directly inquire about Lion Code Technologies' services.
- Blog: Share knowledge through a blog focusing on trends, insights, and best practices in IT and business automation.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#002D72), inspired by Zone24x7, to convey trust and expertise. white, black, red, yellow
- Secondary color: Light blue (#007ACE), mirroring Zone24x7, for a modern and tech-forward feel.
- Accent color: Teal (#00A79D), drawn from Zone24x7, to highlight key interactive elements.
- Headline font: 'Roboto', a modern sans-serif, for clear and impactful headlines (as seen on Zone24x7).
- Body font: 'Open Sans', a legible and friendly sans-serif, ensuring readability similar to Zone24x7.
- Clean and professional icons, consistent with Zone24x7, to represent services and solutions.
- Subtle animations for a polished user experience, inspired by Zone24x7's modern design.
